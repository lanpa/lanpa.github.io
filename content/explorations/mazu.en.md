# Departure
Suddenly felt like going on a pilgrimage. Figured the procession would pass by Changhua Railway Station on the way back, so I took the TRA down.
 - Tze-Chiang Limited Express No. 121, arrived at Changhua at 13:31

{{< figure src="/images/exploration/mazu/IMG_8676.jpeg" caption="Arrived at Changhua; there were shuttle cars provided by kind-hearted people outside the station." >}}


The procession hadn't arrived yet, so I wandered around the city. Noticed traces of Brownian motion: some people go to the restroom, some go to eat, but eventually, the whole group moves in the right direction.
